# Refund policy

Customers can request a full refund within 7 days of delivery for any unused item in its original packaging.

Refunds for orders above 1000 rupees require approval from a support lead before the money is released.

Once a refund is approved, the amount is credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Items marked as final sale, gift cards and digital downloads are not eligible for a refund.

If an order arrives damaged, customers can request a refund within 30 days and do not need to return the item.
