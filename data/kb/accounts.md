# Accounts and login

To reset a password, use the Forgot password link on the login page. A reset link is sent to the registered email and expires after 30 minutes.

Two factor authentication can be enabled from Settings, Security. We support authenticator apps and SMS codes.

An account is locked for 15 minutes after five failed login attempts.

To change the registered email address, verify the current email and then confirm the new one.

Deleting an account removes personal data within 30 days, but invoices are retained for seven years as required by tax law.
