# Shipping and delivery

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days within India. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days.

Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM are dispatched the next working day.

Shipping is free on orders above 499 rupees. Below that a flat fee of 49 rupees applies.

Customers can track an order from the orders page using the tracking ID sent by email once the order is dispatched.

We currently do not ship to international addresses.
